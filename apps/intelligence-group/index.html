About IQ Intelligence Group

The intelligence layer powering the appliance industry.

Our Mission

The appliance industry runs on relationships — between manufacturers who build products, retailers who sell them, and consumers who live with them. But the data connecting these relationships has always been fragmented. Product specs live in PDFs. Training happens once a year. Customer follow-up is tracked on sticky notes. Field reps file reports nobody reads.

IQ Intelligence Group exists to change that. We have built the industry's first connected intelligence platform — a single ecosystem where every product, every interaction, every data point flows into a shared knowledge graph that makes everyone smarter.

The Platform

Our platform spans the entire industry value chain. For consumers, ApplianceIQ.ai provides AI-powered product research. For retailers, our suite of tools covers CRM, training, floor management, and spec building. For manufacturers, we offer field execution intelligence, brand marketing tools, and product information management. And underneath it all, one unified data layer connects everything.

Every product in the ecosystem works standalone — but together, they create an intelligence flywheel that no disconnected point solution can match.

Built in Hamilton, Ontario

IQ Intelligence Group is headquartered in Hamilton, Ontario, Canada. We are building the future of appliance retail from the heart of one of Canada's most dynamic cities.
