The Problem

Manufacturers lack real-time visibility into how their products appear on the sales floor.

🔍

Product Condition Unknown

Displays break, get dirty, or stop functioning — and no one reports it for weeks.

🏷️

Pricing Inaccuracy

Wrong prices on the floor cost sales. You only find out when quarterly numbers miss.

📋

Missing Signage

Promotional materials never make it to displays. Launch campaigns go unexecuted.

⚔️

Competitive Blind Spots

Competitors expand their floor presence while you rely on last quarter's data.

📊

Slow Reporting

Traditional field reports arrive weeks late, inconsistent, and impossible to act on.

🎓

Knowledge Gaps

Retail staff can't sell what they don't understand. Training is sporadic and unmeasured.

The Solution

Every store visit creates verified, actionable intelligence — not just another report.

📸

AI-Assisted Audits

Field reps capture photos and video. AI identifies issues — broken displays, missing signage, pricing errors. Reps confirm before submission.

📊

Brand Execution Scoring

Every visit generates a store score across visibility, condition, signage, pricing, and competitive positioning. Track performance over time.

⚡

Action Management

Every issue becomes a trackable action with assignments, status workflow, time stamps, and full resolution measurement.

🎓

Product Training

Reps train retail staff during visits. Knowledge scores, topics covered, and follow-up needs connect to your dashboard.

🆚

Competitive Intelligence

Floor presence, display sizes, promotions, new models, staff preferences — captured at every visit across every store.

📱

Manufacturer Dashboard

Drill from country to region to retailer to store to product. Real-time issue tracking, resolution metrics, and trends.
