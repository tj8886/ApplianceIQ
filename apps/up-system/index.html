Features

Everything the floor needs. Nothing it doesn't.

No more "whose turn is it?" arguments. No more cherry-picking customers. Just a clean, fair rotation everyone can see.

🔄

Live Up Rotation

Real-time queue shows who's up, who's next, and who's with a customer. Updates instantly across all devices.

👋

Walk-In Logging

Tap to log customers. Auto-assigns to the next rep. Tracks wait time, group size, and whether they asked for someone.

📊

Floor Status Board

See every rep's status: available, with customer, on break, doing admin. Managers see it all from anywhere.

⏱

Shift & Break Tracking

Clock in, clock out. Break and lunch timers with configurable limits. Late/on-time tracking.

🚫

Missed Up Detection

Automatic detection when reps miss their turn. Manager review, excused/unexcused, KPI impact control.

📈

Floor Analytics

Walk-ins per hour, conversion rates, avg greeting time, staffing predictions. Data-driven scheduling.
