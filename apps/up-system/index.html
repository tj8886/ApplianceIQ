Features

Floor intelligence that
actually works.

No clipboards. No whiteboards. No arguments about whose turn it is. Just a clean rotation that runs itself.

🔄

Live Up Rotation

Automatic queue that every rep can see. When a customer walks in, the next rep up gets the notification. No cherry-picking, no skipped turns, no disputes.

👥

Customer Flow Tracking

Log walk-ins, be-backs, phone-ups, and appointments. See traffic patterns by day, hour, and season. Know when to staff up and when to cut.

📍

Floor Coverage Map

Who's with a customer, who's available, who's on break, who stepped out. Managers see the floor from anywhere — phone, tablet, or office screen.

⚖️

Fair Turn Rules

Configurable rotation rules per store. Handle split shifts, appointment-only reps, team leads, and part-timers without manual overrides.

📊

Floor Analytics

Walk-in to close ratio. Average time with customer. Ups taken vs. ups available. Conversion by rep, by shift, by day of week. Real numbers, not guesses.

🔔

Smart Notifications

You're up — phone buzzes. Be-back just walked in — the original rep gets alerted. Manager gets a ping when nobody's on the floor. Quiet when nothing needs attention.

📱

Works on Any Device

Browser-based. No app to install. Works on the phone in your pocket, the tablet on the counter, or the screen behind the register. Set up in 5 minutes.

🏬

Multi-Store Dashboard

Run 2 stores or 20 — see every floor from one dashboard. Compare traffic, conversion, and staffing across locations in real time.

🔗

CRM Integration

Connects to ApplianceIQ CRM. When a rep takes an up, a deal record is created automatically. Coaching scores, recordings, and pipeline — all linked.

How It Works

Set up in minutes. Runs itself after that.

No training needed. If your team can use a phone, they can use the Up System.

01

Add Your Team

Enter your reps' names and shifts. They get a link to join — tap it, and they're on the board.

02

Open the Floor

Manager opens the floor for the day. The rotation starts. Everyone sees who's up on their phone or the floor screen.

03

Take the Up

Customer walks in → rep taps "Take Up" → they're with a customer. The queue advances automatically. No shouting needed.

04

Track & Improve

Every up, every outcome, every minute tracked. Weekly reports show who's converting, when traffic peaks, and where you're losing walk-ins.
