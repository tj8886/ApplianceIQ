Features

Built for the floor.
Not the office.

Large buttons. Minimal typing. Photo-first workflows. Complete a full store visit without a laptop.

📍

GPS Check-In

Arrival time, GPS coordinates, visit purpose, and open issues from prior visits — captured automatically when you start.

📸

AI Photo Analysis

Capture photos by category. AI identifies broken displays, missing tags, dirty units. Confirm or reject each finding.

🔍

Manual Findings

20+ issue categories, 7 condition levels, 17 recommended actions. Record brand, model, severity, and comments.

🎓

Staff Training

Record training topic, models covered, employee names, duration, and knowledge scores. Flag follow-ups.

🆚

Competitive Intel

Competitor floor presence, display sizes, promotions, new models, staff preferences — captured at every visit.

📊

Store Scoring

AI generates a brand execution score at visit completion. See current vs previous score and delta.
