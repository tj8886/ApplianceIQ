Features

Everything the floor needs to know.
Everything leadership needs to see.

One dashboard that speaks every language — from the CEO's annual target to the rep's daily scorecard.

🔬

Corporate → Region → Store → Rep

Four drill-down levels with one click each. Breadcrumb navigation. Dropdown selectors for any region, store, or rep by name. Every metric available at every level — same data, different scope.

💰

Cascading Budgets

CEO enters one growth number. Budget flows to regions, stores, and reps based on historical share. Add or close a store — budget recalculates. Manual override at every level. Math is a suggestion, not a prison.

📊

Margin ($ and %)

Gross margin in dollars and as a percentage — by rep, store, region, corporate. Budget margin targets. See who's protecting margin and who's discounting away the profit.

🛡️

Warranty Intelligence

Attach rate, penetration $ (warranty dollar volume), penetration % (warranty $ ÷ total revenue $ — target 6-7%), opportunities, and warranty revenue — all tracked separately, all budgeted, all by rep.

🏷️

Brand Mix (35 Brands)

Sales % by brand — Samsung, LG, KitchenAid, Sub-Zero, and 31 more. Brands grouped by tier: Entry, Mid, Luxury, Ultra Luxury. Budget brand targets. See who's selling up and who's staying safe.

🏪

Channel Split

Three sales channels: Retail (walk-in), Commercial (builders, property mgmt), Trades (designers, custom homes, cabinet makers). Revenue and % by channel at every level. See which stores are building trade relationships.

🛒

IPO, AOV, Item Value

Items per order, average order value, and average item value. Budget each one. Know who's building multi-piece kitchen packages and who's selling one dishwasher at a time.

🏅

Leaderboard

Podium for top revenue. Warranty champion. Margin leader. 13 category rankings with medals. Auto-generated shoutouts. Small celebrations that keep the floor motivated and competitive.

🏆

AI Coaching Scores

Average coaching score by rep, store, region. Cross-referenced with training completion, revenue, and conversion. Priority coaching flags for reps trending down. Powered by 6 AI coaching personas.

🎓

Training Readiness

Academy completion % by rep and store. Readiness badges. Cross-referenced with coaching scores and revenue — see the direct link between training and performance.

🔄

Floor / Up System

Walk-in count, greeting wait time, floor conversion %, ups taken vs missed. By store and by rep. Know which floors are fast and which are losing walk-ins.

👤

Full Rep Scorecards

One card per rep showing revenue vs budget, margin, warranty attach + penetration, IPO, avg order, coaching score, training %, floor conversion, and channel mix. 15+ metrics in one view.

✏️

Everything Editable

Every metric, every budget, every target — editable at every level. The cascade is a suggestion. Manual overrides win. Every org's metrics are different. Add your own.

🤖

AI Predictions

AI analyzes trends and tells you if you're heading toward or away from budget — before the month ends. Recommends coaching focus. Generates next-year predictions from one growth number.

👁️

Role-Based Access

CEO sees the org. RSM sees their region. Store manager sees their floor. Rep sees their own card. Multi-tenant from day one. Shared views and custom dashboards.
