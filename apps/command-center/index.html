Features

The operation at a glance.
The detail on demand.

Built for owners, VPs, RSMs, and store managers who need to see the whole picture without logging into three different apps.

💰

Cascading Budgets

CEO enters one number — growth % or total target. Budget flows to regions, stores, and reps based on historical share. Adjust at any level. Add or close a store — budget recalculates.

🔬

Drill-Down Views

Corporate → Region → Store → Rep. Every metric available at every level. Tap a store to see its reps. Tap a rep to see their deals, coaching scores, and training progress.

🛡️

Warranty Intelligence

Track warranty by opportunity count AND attach rate. See which reps offer and which close. Budget warranty revenue separately. AI flags reps trending below attach targets.

📊

IPO, AOV, Item Value

Items per order, average order value, and average item value — by rep, store, region. Budget each one. See who's building tickets and who's leaving money on the table.

🏷️

Sales by Brand

Revenue and units by brand across the org. Compare Samsung vs LG vs Bosch at every level. Budget brand targets. See market share shifts.

🤖

AI Predictions

AI analyzes trends and tells you if you're heading toward or away from budget — before the month ends. Recommends where to focus coaching. Generates next-year predictions from one growth number.

📅

Flexible Periods

Load budgets by year, quarter, or month. View actuals for any time period. YoY comparison when multi-year data exists. Fiscal year configurable.

✏️

Everything Editable

Every metric, every budget, every target — editable at every level by the right role. The cascade is a suggestion. Manual overrides always win. Every org's metrics are different.

👁️

Role-Based Views

CEO sees the org. RSM sees their region. Store manager sees their floor. Rep sees their own card. Shared views and custom dashboards. Multi-tenant from day one.
