One platform where manufacturers manage product data and retailers access it — enriched, validated, and always current.

🏭 For Manufacturers

Manage your entire product catalog from one place. Upload videos, manuals, energy guides, CAD files, marketing assets, and publish to the industry.

  • Product images, videos & 360° views
  • Manuals, spec sheets, energy guides & installation docs
  • CAD drawings, Revit families, SketchUp & BIM models
  • Marketing sell sheets, co-op ads & social media kits
  • Rebate programs & warranty management
  • Data quality scoring and validation

🏪 For Retailers

Browse the industry's most complete appliance product database. Get manufacturer-verified specs, images, documents, and CAD files for every product you carry.

  • Always-current product data from the manufacturer
  • Download verified images, documents & CAD files
  • Compare specs across brands and models
  • Access embargoed pre-launch product data
  • Verified pricing, rebates & warranty terms
  • Brand-scoped access — see only what you carry
