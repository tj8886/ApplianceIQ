How It Works

From greeting to lifetime customer in one platform.

Every interaction is tracked. Every follow-up is prompted. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Add contacts and deals

Create individual contacts, buying groups, or business accounts. Link them to pipeline opportunities with value, margin, and expected close date.

Work your pipeline

Drag deals between stages. The system tracks stage duration, flags stale deals, and captures structured close data for every win and loss.

Let AI prioritize your day

The Task Centre surfaces expiring quotes, idle deals, and hot leads. AI coaching personas help you prepare for objections and close strategies.

Deliver and follow through

Won deals automatically generate delivery workflows with tasks for inventory, installation, warranty, and customer satisfaction checks.
