Features

Everything manufacturers need to
see the retail floor clearly.

AI-assisted audits, trained field reps, and a resolution workflow that turns observations into accountability.

📸

AI-Powered Store Audits

Field reps capture photos and video. AI identifies broken displays, missing signage, pricing errors. Reps confirm each finding before submission.

📊

Brand Execution Scoring

Every visit generates a score across visibility, condition, signage, pricing, and competitive positioning. Track trends over time by store, region, or country.

⚡

Action Management

Every issue becomes a trackable action with 15 status stages, time-stamped audit trails, assignments, due dates, and full resolution measurement.

🎓

Product Training

Reps train retail staff during visits. Knowledge scores, topics covered, employee names, and follow-up needs — all connected to your dashboard.

🆚

Competitive Intelligence

Floor presence, display sizes, promotions, new models spotted, staff preferences, customer objections — captured at every visit across every store.

📱

Drill-Down Dashboard

Country → Region → Retailer → Store → Product → Issue. Real-time issue tracking, resolution metrics, service requests, replacement tracking, and CSV exports.

💬

Tiered Comments

Internal, manufacturer, retailer, and service provider comment permissions on every action. Sensitive notes stay private. Shared updates go to the right people.

📦

Service & Replacement Tracking

Service requests, replacement approvals, part orders, technician deployments — every step tracked with time-to-resolution metrics and full audit history.

📤

Export Everything

Actions, findings, visits, training, competitive intel, store summaries — downloadable CSV exports with custom filters by store, status, and priority.
