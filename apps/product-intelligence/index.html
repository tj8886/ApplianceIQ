Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in a seat?
A seat is one user login. Each rep, manager, or admin on the platform needs their own seat. Per-seat products include CRM, Spec IQ, and IQ Academy.
What's the difference between per-seat and per-store pricing?
Products that every rep uses daily (CRM, Spec IQ, Academy) are priced per seat. Products that serve the whole store regardless of team size (Up System, IQ Field, Command Center, Product IQ) are priced per store.
Can I pay annually?
Yes. Annual commitment saves 20% on all retailer products and bundles. Manufacturer contracts are annual by default.
Do you support Canadian pricing?
Yes. All pricing is available in both USD and CAD. Canadian pricing is converted at current rates and rounded for clean invoicing. Contact us for current CAD pricing.
What happens when I run out of AI credits?
AI features pause until your credits refresh next month or you purchase an AI Credit Pack. Credits are shared across all ApplianceIQ products at the company level.
Can I migrate from another CRM?
Yes. Self-service CSV import is free on all plans. For managed migration from Salesforce, HubSpot, or other CRMs, we offer a $1,500 one-time migration service.
What makes the Elite CRM tier different?
The Elite tier includes a Custom AI Coach trained specifically on your company's SOPs, commission structure, brand priorities, and objection handling scripts. It includes an API endpoint so your policies auto-sync into the coach when they change. No other CRM in the appliance industry offers this.
