The Philosophy

Principles that run through every chapter

These are not slogans. They are operating rules built from thousands of customer conversations and every mistake you can imagine.

Discovery Determines Everything

The quality of your recommendation can never exceed the quality of your discovery. Poor discovery leads to guessing. Great discovery leads to confidence.

Solve the Need — Sell the Want

Customers buy because of a need. They choose because of a want. Every recommendation must satisfy both.

Every Channel Is a Showroom

Whether they walk in, text, chat, or video call — the 10 Steps stay the same. The channel changes. The selling does not.

Preparation Beats Luck

Over a week, luck may influence results. Over a year, habits determine results. The best salespeople are not lucky — they are prepared.

Compete Against Your Best

Industry averages describe what is common. They do not describe what you are capable of. Never settle for average.

Humility Creates Trust

Customers do not expect you to know everything. They expect you to care enough to find the right answer. Verification builds more credibility than guessing ever will.

Questions

Who is this for?

Appliance salespeople at any stage — from first day on the floor to store leadership. Independent dealers, chain retailers, and individual salespeople who want to get better.

Do my people really need all 124 chapters?

No. The career path system shows only the chapters relevant to each person's current role. A new hire sees 19 chapters. A floor professional adds 20 more. Nobody reads all 124 unless they are building the program itself.

What makes this different from other sales training?

Two things. First, it was built from 20 years of actual floor experience — not by someone who teaches selling but has never stood behind a counter. Second, the AI coaching is not generic — six specialist personas coach you on the specific material, and your daily scorecard data feeds into the coaching so it knows your actual numbers.

How does AI roleplay work?

The AI plays the customer. You practice the 10 Steps — greeting, discovery, objection handling, closing — in realistic scenarios. You get scored on your performance with specific feedback. Practice your weakest skills without risking a real sale.

What are the certifications worth?

They are not participation trophies. Each certification requires live demonstrations assessed by a real person — your manager, a senior colleague, or a facilitator. You pass by doing the work on the floor, not by clicking answers on a screen.

Can I listen instead of read?

Yes. Every chapter has HD audio narration. Learn on your commute, between customers, or wherever you have headphones and five minutes.

How does team pricing work?

Per-seat, monthly. Every seat gets the full platform — all chapters, AI coaching, roleplay, scorecards, worksheets, and certification. Managers get a dashboard showing their team's progress. The price per seat drops as your team grows.

What if I just want one volume?

The Single Course plan gives you one volume at $297 with lifetime access. Start with Vol IV (Floor Sales Process) or Vol V (Closing & Objections) — those are where most people see the fastest results.
